Exitwise

Privacy

The Exitwise planner runs entirely in your browser.

What is stored

Your projects and any floor plan images you add are saved in this browser's local storage (IndexedDB). Your theme choice and the last project you opened are saved in local storage.

What is sent

Nothing about your buildings leaves your device. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address as with any web request.

Removing your data

Delete projects from the Projects window, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
